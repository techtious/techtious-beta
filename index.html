Deployment model

Agents that run in your infrastructure

01

Connect Your Cloud

We deploy a lightweight agent into your AWS, GCP, or Azure account using a read-write IAM role you control. No data leaves your perimeter.

Within hours, the agent profiles your environment — workloads, spend patterns, config drift, log anomalies — and builds a baseline model.

03

Insights & Actions

Recommendations surface in your dashboard. Approve auto-remediation or act manually. Every action is logged and explainable.

Continuous Optimization

Agents improve over time. As your infrastructure evolves, so does the intelligence — compounding savings and stability every week.
