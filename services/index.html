Platform Engineering

Internal platforms that make your developers fast

Platform Engineering transforms how your engineering teams work. We design and build Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs) that give developers self-service access to the infrastructure and tooling they need — reducing toil and eliminating waiting on ops.

Why this matters
Developer velocity
Self-service environments and golden paths reduce the time from code to production from days to hours.
Reduced cognitive load
Developers focus on product features, not wrestling with infrastructure config or ticketing queues.
Standardisation
Consistent, security-hardened paths for building, testing, and deploying — no more snowflake environments.
Operational excellence
Platform teams own the shared infrastructure layer, freeing product engineers to move faster with confidence.
How we deliver
1
Platform assessment
Evaluate your current developer experience, bottlenecks, and automation gaps.
2
IDP design & build
Architect and implement a developer platform using tools like Backstage, Port, or custom solutions on Kubernetes.
3
Golden path definition
Define opinionated, secure deployment workflows and templates that teams adopt by default.
4
Adoption & enablement
Embed with your teams to ensure the platform is actually used — documentation, onboarding, and feedback loops.

Observability & SRE

Reliability engineering and full-stack observability

We integrate observability and SRE practices to build systems that are reliable, measurable, and self-improving. From real-time monitoring to automation and incident response, we help your teams operate with confidence and ship faster — without compromising reliability.

Why this matters
End-to-end visibility
Monitor infrastructure, applications, and user experience through metrics, logs, and traces in a single coherent system.
Higher reliability
Reduce outages, improve uptime, and ensure predictable performance through SLO-driven engineering.
Faster incident response
Detect, resolve, and learn from failures quickly using automation, alerting, and runbooks your team actually follows.
Balanced innovation
Error budgets and observability insights help teams deliver features faster without risking stability.
How we deliver
1
Assessment & maturity analysis
Evaluate your monitoring systems, reliability metrics, and on-call processes against SRE best practices.
2
Observability implementation
Set up metrics, logs, traces, and dashboards using Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, ELK, or OpenTelemetry.
3
SRE foundations
Define SLIs/SLOs, build error budgets, establish on-call workflows, automation, and incident management.
4
Continuous reliability
Post-incident reviews, chaos testing, performance tuning, and cost-optimised observability at scale.
