Planned capabilities

What DriftGuard will watch

Env Var & Secret Comparison

Compare environment variables and secret references across dev, staging, and prod. Flag any key present in one environment but missing or different in another.

Kubernetes Manifest Diffing

Continuously compare deployed manifests against your source of truth — Helm values, Kustomize overlays, or raw YAML. Surface every undocumented change.

Terraform State Awareness

Watch your Terraform state files for drift between what was planned and what is actually deployed. Alert before unmanaged changes accumulate.

Config & Feature Flag Sync

Track application configuration across environments — including feature flag states. Ensure that what shipped to staging is what ships to prod.

Blast-Radius Scoring

Not all drift is equal. DriftGuard scores each divergence by potential impact — a missing env var for a payment service ranks higher than a log-level difference.

Automated Remediation

For approved drift patterns, DriftGuard can apply the fix automatically — syncing the lagging environment to match the source of truth, with full audit logging.
